# Surface brief: landing page (web/app/page.tsx)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: meditators who lose the breath during screen time. Action: Add to Chrome (placeholder constant until the store listing exists). Proof: the hero illustration itself, the popup shown at real size, the three steps, the philosophy. Constraints: never instruct breathing; no counts, streaks, scores; monochrome ink on cream; no gradients, cards, glass.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The reference sticker brought to life. The breath emblem is the single origin and its flow lines are the page's structure; it refuses the centered-hero-plus-three-cards arrangement.

OWN-WORLD: Cream #F4EBDD paper; ink #1F3B3E (the author's drawing, always pure ink); from the author's earthy swatches: rust #9E5033 as the single action colour, ochre #C9A227 for numerals and one accent line, clay #E1B49A as the desk under stickers; sage #879B82 only for quiet hours. Eczar for display, Mukta for UI. No cards, no rules: sections part by cream voids and by the flow line. Controls drawn in ink: sticker-edged buttons, a hand-drawn pill toggle, a leaf-tipped select.

STORY: A meditator recognizes the sticker, reads "Watch your breath." and is already reminded; understands it is a reminder, not a practice; adds it to Chrome.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop: "Watch" arched over the emblem left of centre, "your breath" below it, flow lines sweeping right across the full width carrying "Just a moment." in script; tagline, Add to Chrome, and How it works sit under the sweep at right. Mobile: Watch / emblem / your breath stacked, flow curling down into tagline and actions. The emblem breathes on a nine-second clock.

FORM: User-pinned: their own illustration (presented in round one as the pick, "The Sticker Sheet"), chosen over two dealt hands; every image is a crop of it. Seed key 6db68fcb.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
